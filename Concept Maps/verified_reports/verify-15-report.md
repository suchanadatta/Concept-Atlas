**Details of new concepts (full definitions and evidences in `verified/verify-15.jsonl` lines 24-31):**
- **`future_state` (Future State)** — In Warburton the doctrine of a future state of rewards and punishments is the religious sanction whose inculcation is proved necessary to civil society from nature and universal consent; all antiquity unanimous that legislators taught it though philosophers disbelieved. Hutcheson makes the same future state after death (happy/miserable per conduct) the only foundation satisfying divine benignity and supporting fortitude. Evidence: Warburton p49 "Containing the Necessity of Religion in general, and of the Doctrine of a future State in particular to Civil Society", p352 "THAT ALL ANTIQUITY WAS UNANIMOUS...FUTURE STATE...NECESSARY TO THE WELL BEING OF SOCIETY", Hutcheson p223 "their future state after death shall be happy or miserable according to their conduct".
- **`paternal_authority` (Paternal Authority)** — Rutherforth distinguishes paternal authority ending when child leaves family, son even acquiring civil jurisdiction over father while filial piety remains, vs Hutcheson on paternal assistance lost when indulgence allowed. Evidence: Rutherforth vol2 p36 "separates himself from the family of his parents, their authority over him is at an end; nay he may even acquire civil jurisdiction over them", Hutcheson p467 "produce a race destitute of all paternal assistance".
- **`positive_law` (Positive Law)** — Hutcheson: "Positive laws are revealed only by words or writing" vs natural laws discovered by reason; Rutherforth: bequest custody and inheritances introduced by positive laws. Evidence: Hutcheson p239, Rutherforth vol1 p12 "it must be some positive law, which, by taking the thing given into its custody", Rutherforth vol2 p67 "where no positive law of the constitution forbids it".
- **`divine_institution` (Divine Institution)** — Newcome's dichotomy: if sacrament were mere humane institution by Jesus as man, remembrance would satisfy; as divine institution requiring Church of England administration it presupposes divine authority. Evidence: Newcome p2 "if the Sacrament was a mere humane Institution, that if Jesus as a Man, and independently of God, instituted it", p4 "If then the Sacrament was a mere humane Institution, we ought not to amuse ourselves", Warburton p31 "giving in his Divine Institutions the last Stroke to expiring Paganism".
- **`civil_society_origin` (Civil Society Origin)** — Rutherforth CHAP II "Of civil society, its nature and origin. I. Civil society what 12 II. The motives, which lead man to form civil societies 13" and majority tacit consent; Warburton makes future state necessary to its well-being. Evidence: Rutherforth vol2 p3 contents, p12 "Civil society what", Warburton p4 "Doctrine of a future State in particular, to Civil Society, from the Nature of Things".
- **`double_doctrine` (Double Doctrine)** — Warburton: "Duplex enim erat doctrinae genus apud antiquas gentes, δημω δες η δπόρρηλον, doctrina vulgaris & doctrina arcana" — same matter treated two ways, legislating sects publicly taught future state/national gods while privately disbelieving, license to lie for public good concerning soul and gods. Evidence: Warburton p367 "right Apprehension of the Nature of the double Doctrine was the true Key to the ancient Greek Philosophy" and Latin, p410 "details out the Doctrine of a future State ... are all of the Exoteric kind".
- **`intellect_moral_sense` (Intellect and Moral Sense)** — Hutcheson index "intellectual, 70 moral sense, roots in, 97-98" and Warburton critique that moral sense school makes "Metaphysics and Logic, by which the Essential Difference...is demonstrated, are nothing but Words" if morality founded only in sense. Evidence: Hutcheson index p628, p620 "on human nature and moral sense, 36n, 43n", Warburton p95.
- **`occupancy` (Occupancy)** — Rutherforth: "Property can now only be acquired by occupancy" and occupancy vs derivative contract; Hutcheson lists occupancy under polity. Evidence: Rutherforth vol1 p48 "Property can now only be acquired by occupancy", p47 "This compaet is either division or occupancy", Hutcheson p??? occupancy under property rights.
