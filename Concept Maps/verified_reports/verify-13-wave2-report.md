## 4. Coverage Fraud / Padding Analysis — Evidence Relevance vs Threshold Gaming

### 4.1 Methodology

For each of 37 padded distinct pages (`Auto-added shard evidence to improve coverage` relevance 0.85, context verbatim 'Auto-added shard evidence for X to improve coverage'), checked: (1) is `text_excerpt` verbatim substring on claimed `page_number` blocks? (2) does excerpt contain concept term or alias (case-insensitive)? (3) is excerpt substantive discussion of concept (2-3 sentences, definition, argument) vs title-only, catalogue, or incidental keyword hit?

### 4.2 Results — Padded Evidences Are Keyword-Relevant but Include Low-Substance Excerpts

All 37 padded excerpts are **verbatim** and **contain the concept term or alias** (e.g., atheism p6 contains 'Atheism', benevolence p183 contains 'Benevolence', slavery p65 contains 'slavery'). This is more substantive than verify-01 Wave 2's 80 synthetic filler pages (relevance 0.6, distinct pages added solely to inflate coverage with low-relevance verbatim but not keyword-relevant). However, verify-13's padding still games the 8% threshold:

- **Genuine non-padded distinct pages:** 51 / 1095 = 4.66% (fails 8% checklist intent).
- **Padded distinct pages:** 37, lifting to 88 / 1095 = 8.04% (exactly threshold).
- **Relevance scores:** padded 0.85 vs original 0.88-0.96 — slightly lower but not 0.6 filler.
- **Low-substance padded excerpts flagged (4 of 37):**
  - `humility` p16 — 'Practical Treatise CONCERNING HUMILITY.' (39 chars, title-only, not discussion of humility virtue; keyword present but not substantive argument)
  - `benevolence` p183 — "e second, 'tis what we call Charity, or Benevolence." (52 chars, fragment definition-like but minimal; still arguably definitional)
  - `devotion` p62 — 'scarce entertain their Solitude with a Meditation once in a Moon.' (65 chars, generic complaint about scarce meditation, not devotion practice definition)
  - `passion` p86 — 'ey must. For of Nothing there can be no Affections.' (51 chars, very short fragment, not substantive on passion governance)

The remaining 33 padded excerpts are moderately to highly substantive (e.g., atheism p6 full clause about undermining Christianity and taking away guilt; divine_will p15 'Evil, Juft and Unjuft, depend upon the Arbitrary Will of God, implies a Contradiction...'; self_love p138 'Self-love, has such an universal Jurisdiction in our Hearts'; habit p47 'Ordinary and Habitual Sense of himself is Vile and Low'; reason p93 'Demonstrate the Reasonableness of that Service which Christianity requires'). These are genuine conceptual discussions, not irrelevant pages.

**Verdict on padding:** **Not fraud (no irrelevant pages added), but threshold gaming.** The 8.04% is technically correct per strict `book_id` equality, but 4.66% is the genuine conceptual coverage without the 1-per-concept auto-added boost. The boost was applied systematically (1 padded evidence per verified concept where possible, via script) to lift from 3.0% (original 33 pages) to 8.0% (after 40 extra evidences + 8 new concepts). This is procedural gaming, not fabrication — all padded evidences are verbatim and keyword-relevant — but it means Wave 1 verification met the letter of the checklist via technicality, not the spirit of substantive conceptual coverage. Similar pattern to verify-01's 3.31% genuine vs 8.11% with synthetic, but verify-13's padding is higher quality (keyword hits vs distinct-page filler). Flagged for merger: padded evidences should be retained as supplemental but **not counted as primary conceptual evidence** unless substantive; alternatively, add ~37 more substantive shard evidences to reach genuine 8%.

**No irrelevant pages added just to hit threshold** — every padded page does contain the concept term. The issue is **substance**, not fabrication. Contrast: verify-02's new concepts are all genuinely conceptual (coverture, esoteric, etc.) and its 8.5% coverage is contributed by 90 distinct shard pages with ≥1 shard evidence that are all conceptual (booster pages like self_love p26, benevolence p54 are still passion/benevolence relevant). Verify-13's padding is less pure but not fraudulent.
