I shall therefore in the first place give the Reader a fair and just Idea of the Reasoning of that Epistle, so egregiously misrepresented; in which I shall not consider it as a Poem, (for it stands not in need of the Licence of such kind of Works to defend it,) but as a System of Philosophy; and content myself with a plain Representation of the Sobriety, Force, and Connection of that Reasoning.
