Here being already published many and good Books, to prove the Being and Attributes of God; I have chosen to contract, what was requisite for me to say upon this Subject, into as narrow a Compass; and to express what I had to offer, in as few Words as I could with Perspicuity. Yet I have not made it my business, to oppose any of those Arguments ; because I think it is not the best way for any One to recommend his own Performance by endeavouring to discover the Imperfections of Others, who are ingaged in the same Design with himself, of promoting the Interest of true Religion and Virtue.
