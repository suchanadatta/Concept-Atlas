The Ingenious Sylvius de le Boe was a Physician, that in searching after the Causes of Distempers gave no small liberty to his prolifick Fancy; yet speaking of those, I am to treat of, he condescended to say, Cognitio rerum naturalium omnium est ab experientia per sensus facta; unde causas effectuum sic compertorum exquirit ratiocinatione concatenata ingenium humanum.
