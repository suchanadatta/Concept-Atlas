Gr. I must submit, but may have still I hope, some presence to your Friendship. You have deserved it Sir, and are welcome to share with us this Days Diversions.

Con. I have ordered some Muliqk with your leave, Madam, we'll em-ploy 'em.

Ph. 'Tis time now to think upon the ceremony that yet remains to make us Maker of our wishes.

Beau. Which perform'd, I resolve to shew those Marry'd Men, whom I have laugh'd out of the fondness, or civility, for their Wives, that I have learn'd by their weakness, how to avoid giving 'em a Revenge, and will so shamelessly boast of loving mine, that 'twill put railery out of countenance; and by preserving my complaisance for her, shew I know how to value my self.

For treating them with rudeness, or neglect, Does most dishonour, on our selves reflect; If that respect which their own Merit drew, We think, by their becoming ours, less due : And as in chusing,we their worth approve, We tax our Judgment, when we cease to love.

FINIS.
